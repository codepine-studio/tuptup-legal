Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

TupTup has no account and no server of its own. The routines you build stay on your device, and — if you are signed in to iCloud — in your own private iCloud storage. We never see them. TupTup asks a child for nothing at all.

TupTup (“the App”) is developed and operated by Oleksii Haidai (CodePine Studio). This Privacy Policy explains what the App stores, what leaves your device, and what choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We operate no servers and receive no information about you, your device, or your child. There is no account, no login, and no profile.

If you subscribe, one third party is involved: RevenueCat, which manages the subscription. It receives a randomly generated anonymous identifier for the installation and whether that installation has an active subscription. It does not receive your name, your email address, your Apple ID, or anything at all about your child or your routines. See section 4.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

To do its job, the App saves the following in its own storage on your device:

• your routines — the name you gave each one, its colour and icon, which step cards you chose, and their order

• which routine is in progress and which step it reached, so a routine survives the screen locking mid‑way

• whether Kid Lock is switched on, and the four‑digit PIN you chose to leave it, if you set one

• when each routine was started and how long it took — the last 500 runs, and a count of completed runs per routine — which is what the statistics on a routine’s page are drawn from

• your language preference, your theme preference, and whether animations and sound effects are switched on, both for the device and for any routine you set differently

• whether the initial setup has been completed, so the App does not offer it again

• a flag noting whether a subscription is currently active, so the App works correctly when offline

The routine names you type are the only free text in the App. The App never asks for your child’s name, age, date of birth, photograph, or anything else about them, and it has no field where a child could type anything.

3. iCloud Sync

If your device is signed in to iCloud, the App mirrors your routines into your own private iCloud key‑value storage. This exists so that reinstalling the App or moving to a new iPhone does not destroy the routines you built.

Only the routines themselves travel this way. The record of when routines were run stays on the device that ran them, and so does the PIN.

That copy lives in your iCloud account, under Apple’s control and Apple’s privacy policy. It does not pass through any server of ours, and we have no ability to read it, retrieve it, or even know that it exists.

If you are signed out of iCloud, the App works exactly the same and nothing leaves your device at all. You can stop the syncing at any time in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud.

4. Subscriptions and Purchases

TupTup is free to use with one routine. An optional annual subscription unlocks unlimited routines. Payment is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details, your name, or your Apple ID.

Subscription status is managed by RevenueCat, Inc., which validates the purchase with Apple and tells the App whether the subscription is active. RevenueCat identifies the installation by a randomly generated anonymous ID that is not linked to any account and that we cannot connect to a person. Its handling of that data is governed by RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Purchases sit behind a parental gate: a question a young child cannot answer. A child holding the phone during a routine cannot reach the purchase screen.

5. Analytics, Tracking and Third Parties

The App contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third‑party tracking. It does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and never shows an App Tracking Transparency prompt, because it has nothing to track.

The only third parties are Apple — for the App Store, payment and iCloud — and RevenueCat, and only if you subscribe.

6. Children’s Privacy

TupTup is meant to be used by a young child, but it is set up, managed and paid for by an adult. This section matters more here than in most apps, so it is worth being precise about it.

The App collects no personal information from children. Specifically, it has:

• no text field, camera, microphone, or recording of any kind that a child can reach

• no chat, no sharing, no social features, no user‑generated content

• no advertising and no third‑party content

• no links out of the App that a child can follow — the links to these documents and the purchase flow are behind a parental gate

The App does keep a record of when routines were run and how long they took, so that a parent can see it on the routine’s page. It is about the routine rather than the child, it names nobody, it never leaves the device — not even to iCloud — and deleting the App deletes it.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has somehow provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it — though, as described above, the App gives them no way to do so.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we hold nothing, there is nothing for us to retain or delete.

Data stored on your device remains there until you delete it. Deleting the App removes the local copy. If iCloud sync was active, a copy may remain in your iCloud account until you remove it in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

If you email us for support, we receive whatever you choose to put in that message, and we keep it only as long as needed to help you.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above.
