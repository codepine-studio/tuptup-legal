1. What TupTup Is, and What It Is Not

It is also not a supervision or safety tool. It does not watch your child, and it cannot tell whether a step was actually done. A young child using the App still needs an adult responsible for them.

This section is the one worth reading twice.

The App has no account and no server. The routines you build are stored on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, mirrored into your own iCloud storage.
